Method notes
  • Baseline CO₂ uses annual electricity demand × baseline electricity factor plus annual heat demand × baseline heat factor.
  • Manual and Z-optimized CO₂ use residual grid electricity, residual heating demand, and annual PV/ST plant emissions from degraded production × kgCO₂e/kWh.
  • PV self-consumption is calculated from the self-consumption setting, PV area, available roof area, and optional battery effect; exported PV receives the feed-in credit.
  • PV and solar-thermal output both degrade annually; solar thermal is additionally multiplied by the usable-fraction curve before reducing heating demand.
  • Investment NPV = initial investment as a negative cashflow plus discounted yearly operating savings, feed-in revenue, and maintenance costs.
  • The Z-optimized scenario minimizes the weighted objective: w prioritizes NPV, while 1 - w prioritizes lifecycle CO₂ emissions.
  • PV lifecycle and cashflow charts are hoverable; the cashflow chart marks break-even when cumulative NPV turns positive.
  • CO₂ factors and cost rates can be adjusted in Expert settings.
