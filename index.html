Method notes
  • Baseline CO₂ = electricity use × grid factor + heat use × heat-carrier factor.
  • PV self-consumption is capped by the building's annual electrical demand.
  • Excess PV earns a feed-in credit and displaces grid electricity at the configured factor.
  • Solar thermal is capped by the declared useful heat load and degrades annually.
  • If heat pump swap is enabled, remaining heat demand becomes electric demand at the selected COP.
  • NPV discounts yearly net cashflows. Abatement cost annualizes CAPEX over the horizon.
  • PV lifecycle chart shows year-by-year kWh output; the amber dashed line marks when cumulative NPV turns positive.
