A web calculator for the Rathaus case study. It combines annual energy inputs, rooftop PV and solar thermal, lighting and heating efficiency, heat-pump conversion, lifecycle emissions, and discounted financial analysis into one responsive dashboard.

Method notes
  • Baseline CO2 = electricity use × grid factor + heat use × heat-carrier factor.
  • PV self-consumption is capped by the building's annual electrical demand after efficiency measures.
  • Excess PV electricity earns a feed-in credit and also counts as displaced grid electricity at the configured factor.
  • Solar thermal is capped by the declared useful heat load and degrades annually.
  • If heat pump swap is enabled, remaining heat demand becomes electric demand at the selected COP.
  • NPV discounts yearly net cashflows. Abatement cost annualizes CAPEX over the horizon.
